# EcoPulse Pro
EcoPulse Pro is a team project developed as part of the ESSE course. It is a web-based platform designed to help users calculate and understand their environmental and carbon footprint.

The website analyzes emissions from different areas such as energy, transport, food, shopping, and waste. It presents the results through interactive charts, comparisons, and real-world offset equivalents like the number of trees needed to offset emissions.
Our team focused on creating a simple, interactive, and user-friendly interface that encourages people to understand their environmental impact and explore ways to reduce it.
